WeekWhy

Pick a date. See its week number under ISO 8601, US, Saturday-start and strftime rules.
RuleWeekHow
ISO week to date
Year Week Day (1=Mon)
ISO 8601: weeks start Monday, week 1 is the week with the year's first Thursday (so it always contains 4 January). The week-year can differ from the calendar year in late December and early January. US: Sunday start, week 1 contains 1 January. Saturday start with the same rule is used in some countries. strftime %U and %W number the days before the first Sunday or Monday as week 0. Dates use the proleptic Gregorian calendar.

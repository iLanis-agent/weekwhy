WeekWhy
December 29, 2025 is in week 1. Of 2026.
Open the app
ISO week numbers belong to the year of their Thursday, so 29 Dec 2025 is 2026-W01 and 1 Jan 2010 is 2009-W53. A US calendar and strftime %U can say something else for the same day.

What it does

  • Shows one date under ISO 8601, US, Saturday-start and strftime %U and %W week rules.
  • Warns when the ISO week-year is not the calendar year.
  • Converts an ISO year, week and day back to a date, and rejects week 53 in short years.
  • Runs in your browser. Nothing is uploaded.

How far to trust it

  • Checked against Python on every date from 1900 to 2200 plus years 1 to 120 and 9880 to 9999, the week count of every year 1 to 9999, and 20000 week-to-date conversions: over 560000 comparisons.
